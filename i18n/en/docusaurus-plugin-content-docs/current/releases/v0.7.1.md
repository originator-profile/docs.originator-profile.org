## OP Inspector

### Fixed

- Fixed an issue where, when `credentialSubject.type` is a Profile Annotation other than `CertificateProperties` (such as `Membership` or `NewsMediaRegistration`), the image specified in the `image` property was not displayed, and a substitute logo was being displayed.
